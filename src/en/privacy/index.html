2. In short

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. What you enter – salary, children, commute – is not transmitted to us or to anyone else and is not stored anywhere. The page sets no cookies and uses no analytics. The only exception is the optional measurement of our ads (section 5), and only with your consent and without your input.

3. Hosting and server

The page runs on servers rented from netcup GmbH, Emmy-Noether-Straße 10, 76131 Karlsruhe, Germany, in its data centre in Vienna, Austria. netcup, and the companies of the Anexia group that work for it, process data only on our behalf and under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

To deliver the page, the server needs to know your IP address for the duration of the connection. We keep no access logs for this page: neither the page's web server nor the upstream entry server stores IP addresses, requested addresses or browser details. Your input to the calculator never reaches the server in any case.

4. Storage in your browser

The page keeps at most two entries in your browser's local storage, which are never transmitted to us or to third parties:

  • bnr-theme: whether you chose the light or dark view, only if it differs from your system setting;
  • bnr-consent: your decision on the measurement of our ads (section 5), for at most twelve months.

Your input to the calculator is not stored, not even in your browser.

5. Measuring our ads with Google Ads

As a non-profit association, OwnYourData advertises this free calculator with Google Ads. To know which ads are useful, we would like to measure whether a visit that started with one of our ads leads to one of these actions: you open our source code on GitHub, or you run a calculation yourself.

Only with your consent. On your first visit, a notice asks whether you agree. Until you accept, nothing is loaded from Google and no request is sent to a Google domain. If you accept, your browser loads the Google Ads tag (gtag.js) from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. We use the tag only for conversion measurement: no Google Analytics, no remarketing, no personalised advertising, and no reports of the pages you view.

What is processed. Only when one of the actions above takes place does your browser send a conversion to Google: your IP address, information about your browser and device, the address and title of the page including the ad click identifier if the address contains one, the time, and which action took place. Your input to the calculator and its results are never transmitted. With the first conversion, Google stores a cookie on our domain, _gcl_au, typically for 90 days, and may read or set cookies on its own domains. In our Google Ads account we see only aggregated figures, not individual visitors.

Legal basis. Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and reading information on your device, § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021, TKG 2021).

Recipient and transfer to the USA. The recipient is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). More in Google's privacy policy and at business.safety.google.

Your choice and how to change it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future, under “Consent settings” at the bottom of each page. When you decline, the _gcl_ cookies on our domain are deleted and the tag is no longer loaded.

When you follow a link, for example to our source code on GitHub or to the association's website, the privacy policy of that provider applies there. The page does not pass on the address you came from (referrer).
